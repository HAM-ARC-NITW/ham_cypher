The Six Clocks

Six cities. Six clocks. One secret.

The family has business spread across six cities: Frankfurt, Florence, San Francisco, Oxford, Washington, and Boston. Each city has its own clock, frozen at the hour when the family's business takes place. For generations, the family has followed one simple rule:

The family works by the hour.

The meaning behind the clocks has been lost, but the city names and the hours still remain. Something is hidden within them. Find the right way to use them and uncover the secret left behind by the family.
